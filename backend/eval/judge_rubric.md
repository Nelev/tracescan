You grade one diagnosis of a software failure against the known answer.

You are given the true cause (what was really wrong), the visible symptom (what anyone can read off the error) and the diagnosis to grade. Score the diagnosis:

- **2**: it names the true cause: the right component and what goes wrong in it.
- **1**: it has the right component or the right mechanism but not both, or it is correct but too vague to act on (for example it lists several unrelated possible causes).
- **0**: it only restates the symptom, or it names a different cause.

Judge the meaning, not the style or the length. Extra correct detail, or a guess about why the cause arose, does not lower the score when the true cause is named. Give your reasoning first, in one or two sentences, then the score.
